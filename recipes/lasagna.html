Description

This is a simple lasagna recipe that is easy to make and delicious to eat. It is a classic Italian dish that is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 16 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the ground beef in a large skillet over medium heat until browned.
  3. Drain the excess fat and return the beef to the skillet.
  4. Add the olive oil, onion, and garlic. Cook until the onion is translucent.
  5. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  6. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  7. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  8. Spread a thin layer of the meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  9. Layer half of the noodles on top of the sauce.
  10. Spread half of the ricotta cheese over the noodles.
  11. Repeat layers, ending with a layer of meat sauce on top.
  12. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  13. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 10 minutes or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
